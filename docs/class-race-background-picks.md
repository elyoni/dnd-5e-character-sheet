# Class / race / background best-fit picks

Reference guide for players picking a class+race+background combo in this
sheet. Scoped to the classes and races this app actually supports
(`CLASS_LIST`/`RACE_LIST` in `dnd_character_sheet.src.html`). These are
strong classic synergy picks, not the only good options.

| Class | Best-fit Race | Best-fit Background | Why | Off-meta but fun |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Barbarian | Half-Orc | Outlander | Con/Str racial bonus + Relentless Endurance keeps you up at 1 HP; Outlander fits the "raised outside civilization" rage archetype | Gnome — a small, furious rage-gnome subverts the archetype and Gnome Cunning still protects your (usually weak) mental saves |
| Bard | Half-Elf | Entertainer | Cha bonus plus two extra skills of choice suits Bard's jack-of-all-trades design; Entertainer reinforces the performer identity | Dragonborn — a booming-voiced draconic bard leans into Bardic performance as literal dragon fear/awe |
| Cleric | Aasimar | Acolyte | Celestial resistance/healing flavor pairs naturally with divine casting; Acolyte is the archetypal temple-trained background | Orc — a battle-cleric of a war god uses Aggressive to close distance, playing against the "frail healer" stereotype |
| Druid | Gnome | Hermit | Gnome Cunning protects your spellcasting mind; Hermit fits the isolated-in-nature backstory | Minotaur — a bull-headed druid who Wild Shapes into delicate forest creatures is a fun contrast |
| Fighter | Dwarf | Soldier | Con bonus and toughness support a frontline martial; Soldier gives the straightforward military background | Fairy — a tiny flying fighter with a longsword bigger than itself, leaning fully into "small but deadly" |
| Monk | Halfling | Hermit | Dex bonus and Lucky trait smooth out AC-less survivability; Hermit fits a monastery upbringing | Dragonborn — breath weapon plus Ki-fueled martial arts makes for a very literal "inner dragon" |
| Paladin | Dragonborn | Noble | Str/Cha racial split matches Paladin's two key stats; Noble suits an oath sworn to a cause or lineage | Tiefling — an infernally-marked paladin fighting their own bloodline's reputation is a classic redemption arc |
| Ranger | Elf (Wood Elf) | Outlander | Dex bonus, extra speed, stealth in nature all reinforce Ranger's wilderness role | Half-Orc — a relentless, brutal tracker who runs prey down rather than out-stealthing it |
| Rogue | Halfling | Criminal | Dex bonus plus Lucky (reroll a nat 1) is excellent for a skill/attack-roll-dependent class; Criminal fits the underworld skillset | Aasimar — a "fallen" celestial working the criminal underworld plays hard against type |
| Sorcerer | Tiefling | Charlatan | Innate Cha bonus plus fire resistance backs up a fire-flavored bloodline; Charlatan plays well with a natural magical "gift" | Dwarf — magic as an unexpected, unwanted inheritance in a famously non-magical culture |
| Warlock | Tiefling | Charlatan (or Hermit for a lonely-pact story) | Cha bonus and infernal flavor line up with a fiend patron; Charlatan for a manipulative pact-user | Halfling — a cheerful, Lucky halfling whose pact patron is something genuinely horrifying is a strong tonal contrast |
| Wizard | Gnome | Sage | Int bonus and Gnome Cunning protect the class's most fragile resource — its mind; Sage is the natural scholar background | Half-Orc — a wizard who leans on Relentless Endurance instead of the usual "avoid melee entirely" playstyle |
| Artificer | Gnome | Guild Artisan | Int bonus plus the tinkerer flavor of Gnomes matches Artificer's craft-magic theme; Guild Artisan fits a trained-crafter backstory | Orc — a battle-forged artificer building weapons for a war-focused, non-technical culture |
